PaceUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PaceUp handles information when you use the PaceUp application and related services.

1. Information We May Handle

Depending on the features you use, PaceUp may process account information such as your email address, authentication information, profile details, fitness activities, progress, and social interactions. The exact information processed depends on the features enabled in the version you use.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to create and secure accounts, provide application features, display activity and progress, support social features, and respond to support requests.

3. Account Security

PaceUp uses authentication and account-verification mechanisms where supported. You should keep your login credentials confidential and contact us if you believe your account has been compromised.

4. Service Providers and Sharing

PaceUp may use service providers to operate its backend, store application data, or deliver account-related emails. Information may be processed by these providers only as needed for their services, subject to the applicable arrangements and law.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the service and meet applicable legal or security requirements. Available account-deletion options and the handling of associated information will be described here once the application's deletion process has been verified.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Contact us to make a privacy request.

7. Children's Privacy

PaceUp is not intended to be used in violation of applicable age requirements. Contact us if you believe information has been provided inappropriately by a child.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as PaceUp develops. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact paceuppu@gmail.com.
